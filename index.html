4. Emergency Coordination Instructions

  • Prioritize incidents marked Critical, then High.
  • Coordinate rescue teams for stranded residents.
  • Direct evacuees to centres with available capacity.
  • Replenish supplies at centres with low stock.
  • Confirm response completion before resolving an incident.
